SIMMONS MEDICAL PRACTICE
SMP Problem Analysis
Information provided directly by Dr Simmons
1
Ageing, Unreliable Network
Outages hit reception, consulting rooms and the visiting specialist's room alike — bulk-billing claims can fail mid-consult when the connection drops.
2
IT Contract Ending in 1 Month
The current support arrangement concludes within weeks, leaving a hard deadline to transition without disrupting patients or staff.
3
Disconnected Accounting
Accounting software sits apart from the patient management system, duplicating data entry for billing and reconciliation.
4
No Automated Reminders
The patient management system can't trigger SMS/email reminders, contributing to avoidable missed appointments.
5
No Proactive Technology Advice
Investment decisions are reactive rather than planned — there's no standing forum for forward-looking guidance.
